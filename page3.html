Welcome to Page 3

So you made it past the dragon, I see. Now you have to face the final boss! It's a math problem that has never been solved. If you solve it you can take down the website. If not, you'll be trapped here forever. Here you go.

Nobody has ever beaten this one. Three mathematicians looked at it, went very quiet, and then moved to the countryside to farm goats. That is the effect this problem has on people.

You get one attempt. There is no going back, there is no hint button, and your stick from Page 2 will absolutely not help you here. Take a deep breath and scroll down.

Rules of the Final Battle

  • You may not use a calculator, an abacus, or your fingers.
  • You get exactly one attempt at the answer.
  • Guessing "42" does not count and the boss will know.
  • You are not allowed to close the browser tab to escape.
  • If you win, the entire website shuts down permanently.
