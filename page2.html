Welcome to Page 2

So this is Page 2 of my story. Now we jump over the hole. I'm surprised you made it this far, but now you have to beat the dragon that shoots fire. You only have a stick, so good luck!

The dragon's name is Gerald. He has been guarding this path for six hundred years and he is extremely bored, which somehow makes him more dangerous, not less. He has burned down four villages purely out of boredom.

Your stick is roughly two feet long and slightly damp. This is not ideal against a fire-breathing lizard the size of a bus, but I believe in you. Sort of. Head to Page 3 when you are done, assuming you are still alive.
